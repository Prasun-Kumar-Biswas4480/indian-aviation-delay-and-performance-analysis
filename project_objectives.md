## 📋 The 18 Project Objectives (One-Line Summary)

1. **Carrier Punctuality:** Benchmark official On-Time Performance (OTP %) across all scheduled domestic airlines.
2. **Passenger Complaints:** Quantify leading drivers of consumer dissatisfaction on the MoCA AirSewa grievance portal.
3. **Longitudinal Punctuality:** Track multi-year monthly punctuality trajectories across carriers from 2021 to 2026.
4. **Complaint Distribution:** Rank domestic carriers by total consumer grievances lodged against operational scale.
5. **Diurnal Bottlenecks:** Identify peak arrival delay hours and bottleneck days of the week from booking records.
6. **Fare vs. Delay:** Evaluate the statistical correlation between commercial ticket pricing and arrival delay minutes.
7. **Busiest Corridors:** Rank India's top 15 domestic flight routes by annual passenger volume using DGCA Table 5.01.
8. **Directional Flow:** Assess symmetry between inbound and outbound passenger traffic across all 835 city pairs.
9. **Seat Occupancy:** Benchmark weighted Passenger Load Factor (PLF %) distributions across reporting airlines.
10. **Market Dominance:** Analyze domestic passenger market share concentration and sector dominance under Table 4.01.
11. **Fleet Evolution:** Visualize multi-year carrier fleet evolution across load factor and average flight distance.
12. **Traffic Trajectory:** Measure industry growth trajectory and recovery index relative to the 2019 pre-COVID baseline.
13. **Volume Competition:** Animate year-by-year passenger volume competition across scheduled airlines (2018–2024).
14. **Capacity Recovery:** Contrast post-lockdown seat deployment (ASK) against real passenger demand (RPK).
15. **Unit Economics:** Evaluate carrier unit profitability by comparing Cost (CASK) against Revenue (RASK).
16. **Corporate Solvency:** Assess absolute airline operating revenues against expenditures under Form EF filings.
17. **Cost Breakdown:** Determine the proportion of operating expenditure driven by Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF).
18. **Research Synthesis:** Provide empirical conclusions on Tata privatization, LCC productivity, and pandemic recovery.

## 📌 Examiner Takeaway

* **100% Traceability:** Every objective is stated in a single clear line and mapped directly to a dashboard visualization.
* **Dual-Horizon Grounding:** Combines genuine **Ministry of Civil Aviation (MoCA) daily OTP & AirSewa grievance monitoring** with **DGCA annual statistical abstracts** and extensive flight booking records.
* **Interactive Evaluation:** Evaluators can filter by era or airline, toggle Tata Group consolidation, and run animated transitions directly on the dashboard.
