OUR STEP-BY-STEP DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY
========================================================================================

STEP 1: FINDING & AUDITING OUR DATA
- We searched through our workspace for aviation data and identified:
  1. Over 1,300 government Excel files published by India's DGCA in 'yearly statistics/' and 'monthly statistics/':
     • Table 1 Series (1.01–1.08): Individual airline operational reports.
     • Table 2 Series (2.01–2.08): National totals combining All Scheduled Indian Airlines.
     • Table 3 Series (3.01–3.08): Private sector totals combining All Private Airlines.
     • Table 4.01: Official annual domestic passenger market share filings.
     • Table 5.01: 835 city-to-city route records.
     • Forms D & EF / Forms 1A–1G: Corporate financial and fleet returns.
  2. Two large flight booking datasets: 'economy.csv' (217,054 rows) and 'business.csv' (98,220 rows), giving over 315,000 real flight records showing dates, routes, flight numbers, and ticket prices in Rupees.
  3. Ministry of Civil Aviation (MoCA) Daily Operational Reports ('india-aviation-traffic-main/aggregated/daily.csv'): 1,299 daily snapshot records covering official carrier On-Time Performance (OTP %) and AirSewa passenger complaints from 2020 through 2026.

STEP 2: FILTERING OUT UNWANTED DATA
- We wrote rules to keep our data clean:
  • Filtered out dedicated cargo airlines (Blue Dart Aviation and Quikjet) so freight operations don't distort passenger load factors.
  • Removed government VIP helicopters and private charters (Table 6 series) because they don't operate on regular commercial flight schedules.
  • Fixed messy airline names in the raw tables (converting 'AIR_INDIA' to 'Air India', and standardizing AirAsia India and Air India Express to 'AIX Connect').
  • Grouped smaller regional airlines (Alliance Air, Star Air, Fly91, Flybig, IndiaOne Air) under 'Regional Carriers' so graphs stay readable.

STEP 3: BUILDING THE DATA PREPARATION PIPELINE
- Reading 1,300+ Excel files directly inside a web app was very slow. We created 'preprocess_dgca.py' to scan all Excel files once and save clean summary CSVs:
  • 'macro_data_clean.csv' (78 rows of annual operational stats extracted from Table 1.01 files)
  • 'monthly_macro_data_clean.csv' (120 rows of monthly operational stats from Tables 1.02–1.08, 2.xx, and 3.xx)
  • 'statutory_data_clean.csv' (59 rows of corporate financial data from Forms D & EF / 1A–1G)
- Built 'pipeline/etl.py' to load these clean files, read the flight booking records, parse Table 4.01 (market shares), and parse Table 5.01 (835 city-to-city flight routes).
- Added Akasa Air for post-2022 coverage so it appears across all filters.

STEP 4: GETTING THE AVIATION MATH RIGHT
- Implemented all math formulas in 'pipeline/metrics.py':
  • 1,000,000 Money Scaling: In government Form EF filings, airline finances are written in "Millions of Rupees". We multiplied expenses and revenues by 1,000,000 to derive accurate CASK and RASK per seat-kilometer.
  • Simpson's Paradox Guard: Calculated weighted PLF (sum of RPK / sum of ASK * 100) to prevent small regional routes from biasing national averages.
  • Zero-Clipped Arrival Delays: Set early arrivals to 0 minutes on summary cards and heatmaps so early touchdowns do not conceal the severity of late arrivals.
  • Zero-Division Checks: Added guards (`if denom == 0: return 0`) across all functions.

STEP 5: BUILDING THE STREAMLIT WEB DASHBOARD
- Created 'app.py' using Streamlit and organized the project into 6 tabs:
  • Tab 0: Flight Operations, Punctuality & Grievances (Official MoCA OTP %, AirSewa complaint distributions, diurnal delay heatmap, fare vs delay scatter).
  • Tab 1: Network Density & Corridors (Top 15 routes led by Delhi–Mumbai with 6.85M passengers, and directional flow balance).
  • Tab 2: Fleet Productivity & Capacity (Load factors, IndiGo's 60%+ market share, and an animated bubble chart).
  • Tab 3: Pre- vs Post-COVID Trajectory (The 60% dip in 2020, recovery beyond 110% in 2024, and the animated racing bar chart).
  • Tab 4: Corporate Financial Health (CASK vs RASK profitability comparison, revenue vs expenses, and jet fuel cost breakdown).
  • Tab 5: Empirical Conclusions (Plain-English answers to our 3 university research questions).
- Added responsive sidebar filters: Operating Era, Carrier Multi-Select, Diurnal Time Windows, and the 'Tata Group Consolidation' toggle.
